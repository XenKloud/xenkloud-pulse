2. How we protect AWS credentials specifically

  • Credentials are encrypted before being stored, using industry-standard symmetric encryption.
  • Only automated processes required to generate your reports access the decrypted values — not stored or transmitted in plain text at rest.
  • We request read-only access only. We never ask for or require credentials capable of modifying your infrastructure.
  • When you cancel your subscription, we deactivate further processing and delete your stored credentials within [30] days upon request.

3. How we use your information

We use your information solely to operate the Service: generating and delivering your reports, processing payment via Stripe, responding to support requests, and improving the product based on feedback. We do not sell your data to third parties.

4. Third parties we rely on

  • Stripe — payment processing. See Stripe's own privacy policy for how they handle payment data.
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) — your own cloud account, accessed via the read-only credentials you provide.
  • Our email provider (IONOS) — used to deliver reports and respond to inquiries.

5. Data retention

We retain your account information for as long as you remain subscribed, and for a reasonable period afterward for accounting and legal purposes. AWS credentials are deleted upon request following cancellation.
