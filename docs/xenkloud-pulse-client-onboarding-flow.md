# Welcome to Xenkloud Pulse — What Happens Next

Thanks for signing up. Here's exactly what happens from here, step by step.

---

## Step 1: Payment

You'll receive a secure payment link for your **$29/month Founder plan**. Once payment is confirmed, we start setup right away.

*(No long-term contract — cancel anytime, effective at the end of your current billing month.)*

## Step 2: Connect your AWS account (5 minutes, read-only)

We'll send you a separate short guide — **"Connecting Your AWS Account to Xenkloud Pulse"** — with exact click-by-click steps to create a **read-only** access key. This means:

- We can see your billing and usage data
- We can **never** change, delete, or create anything in your infrastructure
- You can revoke access at any time in a few clicks

Send the access key back to us securely (we'll tell you how in that guide).

## Step 3: We build your first report (within 24 hours)

Once we have your read-only access, we run your account through Xenkloud Pulse and prepare your first report. This includes:

- What you've spent this month vs. your budget, and whether you're on pace
- Any unusual cost spikes and what caused them
- A prioritized list of ways to save money, ranked by ease and impact

You'll receive this as a **PDF report** by email, along with a short personal note. If you'd like to walk through it live, just reply and we'll set up a 15-20 minute call.

## Step 4: Ongoing monitoring

After the first report, Xenkloud Pulse keeps watching your account. Going forward, you'll receive:

- A fresh report each month, same format
- A heads-up email any time we spot something unusual worth flagging sooner than your next monthly report

## Step 5: Acting on recommendations

Every report includes clear, ranked fixes. You're always in control of what you act on:

- Handle any fix yourself using the steps we provide, **or**
- Reply and ask us to implement it for you (may involve a separate scope/quote depending on complexity)

Just reply to any of our emails directly — no ticket system, no support queue. You're talking straight to the person doing the work.
